## Teaching

During the course of my PhD, I teached for ~ 200 h accross various subjects at the undergraduate level, in the [Physics Department](https://fst-physique.univ-lyon1.fr/formation/licence) of Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1. I teached thermodynamics lab to first years, where we focused on building an experimental protocol and evaluating the uncertainties associated with each measurement. I teached general physics lab (optics, electromagnetism, dynamics...) in third year, with a focus on computer-assisted measurements and analysis, as well as scientific writing and oral communication of results. I finally teached a introductory course to astrophysics to second year students, mixing lecture materials with tutorials. The materials went from observational science (coordinate systems, magnitudes, dust) to physics of astronomical objects (dynamics of 2-body systems and galaxies, stellar evolution) and cosmology. This was a particularly pleasant course to teach as it allowed me to link it directly with my research subject. 

## Outreach

I also enjoy communicating my science with the broader public. I did a small talk presenting my PhD subject to undergraduate students as part of the [International Day of Women and Girls in Science](https://egalite-diversite.univ-lyon1.fr/2023/12/12/journee-des-femmes-et-filles-de-science/). I also presented a broad overview of cosmology with Type Ia Supernovae at [Astro on Tap](https://www.instagram.com/p/DMHzDjUs3Xb/?igsh=MW5mZGR1bTZrcXFlMA==), where astronomers present their work to the general public in a pub.
