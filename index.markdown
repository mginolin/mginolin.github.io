I am a researcher in observational cosmology, interested in Type Ia supernovae. I did my PhD (2022-2025) at the Institut de Physique des 2 Infinis in Lyon, France. I am currently a Research Associate at the Kavli Institute for Cosmology, Cambridge.

My main area of research focuses on how Type Ia supernovae depend on the astrophysical environment they explode in, and how these dependencies may affect cosmological paremeter inference. To study this issue, I am part of the Zwicky Transient Facility ([ZTF](https://www.ztf.caltech.edu/)) collaboration, as well as the [Dark Energy Science Collaboration ([DESC](https://lsstdesc.org/)) of LSST, and the Time-Domain Extragalactic Survey (TiDES), which is part of [4MOST](https://www.4most.eu/cms/science/exgalconsurv/). I am also interested in how to simulate Type Ia supernovae, and as such is one of the main contributor the of the [skysurvey](https://skysurvey.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) library.
